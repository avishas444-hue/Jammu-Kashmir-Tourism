About Jammu and Kashmir

Jammu and Kashmir is a region in the northern part of India, known for its extraordinary natural beauty and cultural diversity. The region is surrounded by magnificent Himalayan mountains and is home to famous destinations such as Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg. It is also known for its traditional Kashmiri handicrafts, Pashmina shawls, Paper Mache art, saffron and beautiful gardens. The combination of natural beauty, historical heritage and rich traditions makes Jammu and Kashmir a special destination for travellers.

Why Visit Jammu and Kashmir?

  • Beautiful snow-covered Himalayan mountains.
  • Famous lakes and traditional Shikara rides.
  • Green valleys and colourful Mughal gardens.
  • Ancient temples and historical monuments.
  • Traditional handicrafts and Pashmina shawls.
  • World-famous Kashmiri saffron.
  • Rich culture, traditions and delicious food.
