Discover the Traditional Shopping of Kashmir

Jammu and Kashmir is famous for its beautiful handicrafts, traditional textiles, aromatic spices and artistic products. The markets of Kashmir reflect its rich culture, creativity and heritage. From luxurious Pashmina shawls to colourful Paper Mache art and world-famous saffron, the region offers many unique shopping treasures.

01. Traditional Kashmiri Pashmina Shawl

Pashmina is one of the most famous traditional textile products of Kashmir. It is admired for its softness, warmth and delicate craftsmanship.

About Pashmina Shawls

Pashmina shawls are traditional luxury textiles associated with Kashmir. They are made from fine cashmere wool obtained from the undercoat of Changthangi goats. These shawls are known for their softness, lightweight texture and excellent warmth.

Special Features

  • Made from fine and soft cashmere fibres.
  • Lightweight, warm and comfortable.
  • Known for delicate embroidery and designs.
  • Available in different colours and patterns.
  • Represents the traditional craftsmanship of Kashmir.

Uses

Pashmina shawls are used as winter accessories, elegant clothing items and special traditional gifts. They are also valued as examples of Kashmiri textile art.

02. Kashmiri Paper Mache Handicrafts

Paper Mache is a beautiful traditional art form of Kashmir. It is famous for colourful patterns, floral designs and detailed hand painting.

About Paper Mache

Paper Mache is a traditional handicraft technique in which paper pulp is shaped into decorative objects. After drying, the objects are painted with beautiful colours and detailed artistic designs. Kashmiri artisans are especially known for their delicate floral patterns.

Special Features

  • Made using paper pulp and traditional techniques.
  • Decorated with colourful hand-painted designs.
  • Features floral patterns and artistic details.
  • Represents the creativity of Kashmiri artisans.
  • Available in many decorative shapes and sizes.

Uses

Paper Mache products are used as decorative showpieces, jewellery boxes, trays, bowls, vases and beautiful souvenirs. They are popular gifts for visitors.

03. Kashmiri Saffron – The Golden Spice

Kashmir is famous for its high-quality saffron, a valuable spice known for its rich aroma, natural colour and culinary importance.

About Kashmiri Saffron

Saffron, commonly known as Kesar, is obtained from the dried red stigmas of the flower Crocus sativus. Kashmir is well known for saffron cultivation, especially in the Pampore region. It is valued for its distinctive aroma, colour and flavour.

Special Features

  • Obtained from the red stigmas of Crocus sativus.
  • Known for its pleasant aroma and rich colour.
  • Used as a natural food colouring and flavouring.
  • Requires careful harvesting and processing.
  • Kashmiri saffron is a famous regional speciality.

Uses

Saffron is used in traditional dishes, sweets, desserts, milk preparations and special festive foods. It is an important part of Kashmiri culinary culture.
